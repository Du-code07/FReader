FReader DOCX reader

OpenDocument.core 7.7.0 is bundled from the official @opendocument/odr-core
npm package. The WASM engine is unmodified. The JavaScript module was renamed
from .mjs to .js for Android MIME compatibility; the wrapper import was adjusted.
The original wrapper is included as engine/index.upstream.js. Its HTML output is adapted for FReader's
existing read-only UI. No document is uploaded, edited or saved by this viewer.

The host reuses the Noto Sans KR, Noto Serif KR and Latin Modern Math fonts
already bundled under assets/hwp/fonts. Their licenses and notices are included
there and in assets/hwp/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt. Missing document fonts are
substituted. FReader paginates the rendered content to the document's page size;
the resulting pagination and complex Word features can differ from Microsoft Word.
