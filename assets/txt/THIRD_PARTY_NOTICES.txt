FReader offline TXT support

TXT bytes are decoded by Dart Unicode codecs and cp949_codec 1.0.2 (BSD-3-Clause).
The local FReader adapter writes bounded EPUB chapters using archive 3.6.1 (MIT).
They are displayed by the existing flutter_epub_viewer 2.2.1 (BSD-3-Clause),
which embeds Epub.js 0.3 (the bundled build) (BSD-2-Clause). Epub.js is an EPUB renderer;
TXT decoding/conversion is supplied by FReader, not by Epub.js itself.
No account, remote document upload, payment, or runtime network download is required.
The original TXT file is never modified.

See the accompanying license files and PROVENANCE.json for upstream sources.
